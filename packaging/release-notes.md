**v1.3.1 — the game's story titles; smaller packs**

- **Story titles:** the player's title bar, the end of a story and its "Next" button showed each story's internal name, a staff label the game never shows ("剣姫、ここに参上（Sのみ）", "戦いの渦中（バトル前）") that the global client left in Japanese for most stories. They now show the title the game shows, as the story lists already did ("Yearning Figure" / "あこがれの後ろ姿"), with (1/2), (2/2) when one stage plays several stories. Stories that belong to no stage (the Others list) keep their name.
- **Smaller packs:** builds made before the official gacha name plates were found drew a plate for every card, and later builds never removed them, so every pack for the Android app carried about 100 MB of plates that are never shown. A rebuild now removes them.

**Updating:** click Rebuild once — the titles are written when the player is built. Windows: replace `DanMemoPlayer.exe` and the `_internal` folder, keep your `work` folder, rebuild (and pack again for the phone). Android: install the new APK over the old one; if you play from a pack, rebuild and pack again on the computer as well.

No game data or keys are included; packs hold your own game data, so keep them to your own devices.

**日本語**：
